Tapitus — Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what Tapitus does and does not do with your information.

1. What We Collect

Tapitus collects almost nothing. Your recorded tap sequences (constellations) are stored locally on your device only. They are never shared with anyone.

2. Accessibility Service Data

Tapitus uses Android's Accessibility Service to perform taps on your screen. This service does not collect, read, store, or transmit any information about the content of your screen, your personal data, your accounts, or your activity on other apps.

3. Payment Information

If you subscribe, payment is handled entirely by a third party and processed in accordance with their policies and/or terms. Tapitus never sees, stores, or has access to your credit card number or banking information.

4. Device Information

Tapitus uses an anonymous device identifier to verify your subscription status. This identifier is transmitted to our secure backend server solely for the purpose of confirming your subscription. It is not linked to your personal identity, not shared with any third party, and not used for any other purpose.

5. Users and Age

We do not knowingly collect personal information from any user without consent. Users under age 13 must have prior permission from a parent or appropriate guardian before using this app.

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated. Continued use of Tapitus after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact
